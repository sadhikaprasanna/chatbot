This article explains how to export your bookmarks to an HTML file for backup or importing into another web browser.

Here are the differences between a backup and an export:

* **Backup (JSON/JSONLZ4):** Used for restoring bookmarks in Firefox only (keeps structure, can overwrite).
* **Export (HTML):** Used for transferring bookmarks to other browsers or computers.

**Note:** To do this on mobile, you must use Firefox Sync. See How do I set up Sync on my computer? to learn how to set up Sync and connect additional mobile devices.

First, make sure Firefox is up to date. To do this, **[macOS]** select Firefox in the Menu bar at the top of the screen, choose About Firefox **[Windows / Linux]** click the menu button  at the right side of the toolbar, click Help Help and Report , select About Firefox  and click **Restart to update Firefox** if the option appears. Then follow these steps:

1. Click the menu button  to open the menu panel.
   Click Bookmarks and then click the Manage bookmarks bar at the bottom.
2. In the toolbar of the *Library* window click **[macOS]** the  *Import and Backup* button

     Import and Backup  and choose Export Bookmarks to HTML… from the drop-down menu.**[Windows]**
    **[Linux]**
3. In the *Export Bookmarks File* window that opens, choose a location to save the file, which is named bookmarks.html by default. The desktop is usually a good spot, but any place that is easy to remember will work.
4. Click Save. The *Export Bookmarks File* window will close.
5. Close the *Library* window.

*Your bookmarks are now successfully exported from Firefox. The bookmarks HTML file you saved is now ready to be imported into another web browser or a new device.*

If you don’t see your bookmarks on the bookmarks bar after importing:

1. Click the menu button  to open the menu panel.
2. Click More tools.
3. Select Customize toolbar…
4. Click Toolbars located at the bottom-left of the window.
5. Select Bookmarks Toolbar and click Always Show.
6. Click the Done button located at the bottom-right of the window. A bookmarks bar with your saved bookmarks will appear below the toolbar.
