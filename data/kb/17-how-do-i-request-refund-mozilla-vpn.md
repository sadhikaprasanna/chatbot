We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee, so you can try Mozilla VPN risk-free.

If you purchased Mozilla VPN through an app market like the App Store, your purchase is subject to that app market's rules and might not qualify for a refund.

Please note that, for users who purchased Mozilla VPN through the Google Play Store, the support team will require your order ID to process your refund. See Find your Google Store receipt & order number in case you don’t know your order ID.

**Note:** If you purchased the Mozilla VPN subscription through Apple App Store your purchase is subject to the store's terms and conditions for terminations.

# Request a refund through your Mozilla account

1. Access your account at https://accounts.firefox.com/signin.
2. In the panel to the left, under Settings, select **Paid subscriptions**.
3. Find the Active subscriptions section.
4. Click Get Help.
5. On the Mozilla Support page, select Contact Support.
   * Under *What are you contacting us about?*, select **Other**.
   * Enter **Refund Request** in the *Subject (optional)* field.
   * Explain why you are requesting a refund in the *Description of the issue* field.
   * Click the Send button.

# Request a refund through the Mozilla VPN app

1. Open Mozilla VPN and click (or tap) the Settings  icon.
2. Scroll down and click (or tap) Help.
3. Click (or tap) Contact support.
4. Fill out the form with your request by completing the following fields:
   1. Enter **Refund Request** in the *Subject (optional)* field.
   2. Explain why you are requesting a refund in the *Description of issue* field.
   3. For Google Play purchases, please include your Order ID.
5. Click (or tap) the Send button.

You’ll see a refund back to your card in 5-10 business days.

# Request a refund through the Mozilla VPN support Knowledge Base

1. Visit the Mozilla VPN support Knowledge base and click **Get support** in the banner that appears at the bottom of the page.
2. In the *Mozilla VPN Solutions* page, click **Contact Support** in the banner to the right.
3. You'll be prompted to sign in with your Mozilla account to be able to contact support or request a refund.
4. Fill in the **Contact Support** form.
5. Under *What do you need help with?*, select **Payments & Billing**.
6. Enter **Refund Request** in the *Subject* field.
7. Click Submit.

Our team will get back to you as soon as possible.
